**SLS Mass Notifications 0.1.5-beta — Available now**

SLS Mass Notifications 0.1.5-beta is available for FreePBX 17.

- Up to 10 paging groups with individual PINs, saved SIP text and approved external-IVR callers.
- A separate operator portal with scoped access and mandatory TOTP.
- Saved sites/device audiences, calendar scheduling and clearer delivery results.
- Exact desktop receipts, steadier live streams and external speech after each call answers.
- AES-256 configuration encryption, annual key rotation and FreePBX backup/restore.
- Speech/image/SMS previews, multilingual voices and richer weather emails.

**🧪 Labs, disabled by default:** SMS/MMS, SAML/OIDC, directory sync, SIP speakers, signed sensors, floor plans, drills, shift routing, two-person approval, meetings and notification continuity.

Dangerous failover/mirroring, door actions and public-warning submission are **NON-PRODUCTION LABS ONLY**. Provider/device and multi-host testing remain necessary; this is not full PBX failover.

Minor fixes are covered in the release notes and commit history.
